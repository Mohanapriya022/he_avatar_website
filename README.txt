AVATAR — HOUSEHOLD PEST CONTROL WEBSITE (multi-page)

Pages: index.html (Home) · products.html (Our Products) · about.html · enquiry.html · contact.html
OPEN: unzip and open index.html. No framework needed.

SLIDERS: banner images from images/photos/. Edit the SLIDES list at the top of script.js (image, label, link, pest).
ICON STRIP: pest icons (mosquito, cockroach, fly, ant, rat) under the slider; click to jump to a matching banner.
PRODUCT CARDS: images/products/<product-id>.jpg (split/cropped from your photos on a blurred backdrop).
PRODUCT PAGES: products.html#household/<id>. Edit the HOUSEHOLD list in script.js for names, text and images.
