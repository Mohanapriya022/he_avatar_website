Trusted protection for every home.

HE Avatar offers a focused range of household pest-control products that help families keep their homes safe, clean and comfortable. The range covers mosquito and cockroach sprays, chalk, coils, liquid vaporizers, electric racquets, gels and rat control.

We believe the right product starts with understanding the pest. Every product page explains which pest it targets, where and how to use it, and which precautions to follow, so customers, dealers and retailers can choose with confidence.
